QUESTIONS

Before your visit

How do I arrive discreetly?

Park on level B2 and take the private lift to the twenty-first floor. Give your booking name at the panel and the doors open to your suite.

Can I request a female or male therapist?

Yes, and the request is held permanently on your file once you make it.

Do you accommodate couples?

Three suites are set for two guests, with a shared terrace. Mention it in the message field.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free until four hours before. Inside that window we hold half against your next visit rather than charge it.

Are gift certificates available?

Yes, in any value or by treatment, delivered by courier in the emirate or digitally the same day.
