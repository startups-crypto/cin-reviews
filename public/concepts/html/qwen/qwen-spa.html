Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring to my appointment?+
Nothing but yourself. Robes, slippers, towels and amenities are provided. We recommend arriving 15 minutes early to enjoy the relaxation lounge before your treatment.
Can I reschedule or cancel?+
Yes — complimentary changes up to 24 hours before your booking. Inside that window a 50% fee applies, as we hold the suite exclusively for you.
Do you offer couples treatments?+
Our Private Duo Suite hosts side-by-side sessions with shared hammam access. Couples packages include champagne and a guided breathing ritual.
Are products suitable for sensitive skin?+
Absolutely. Every protocol uses hypoallergenic, patch-tested formulas, and our therapists adjust pressure, temperature and scent during the session.
